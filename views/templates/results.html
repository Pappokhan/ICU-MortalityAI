The five partitions are stratified splits of one dataset, not independent hospitals. The ensemble averages predicted probabilities; it is not parameter-averaging FedAvg.

Risk bands: Low < $t_youden (Youden's J, high-sensitivity cut-off) · Moderate $t_youden–$t_f1max · High ≥ $t_f1max (F1-maximising cut-off). Models were trained on a SMOTE-balanced set, so probabilities are not calibrated to real-world mortality — interpret the bands, not the raw percentages.
